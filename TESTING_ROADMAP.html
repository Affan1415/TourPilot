👥 Staff Management Workflow

TC-SA-001: Create Staff Member with Role Assignment Critical
  1. Navigate to /dashboard/staff
  2. Click "Add Staff" button
  3. Fill in: name, email, phone, role (admin/location_manager/front_desk/captain/affiliate)
  4. Assign to a location
  5. Set reporting manager (reports_to)
  6. Submit form
Expected: Staff created, invitation email sent, appears in staff list with correct role badge
API: POST /api/staff

  • Can admin create another admin? Should this be restricted?

  • Circular reporting structure (A reports to B, B reports to A)
